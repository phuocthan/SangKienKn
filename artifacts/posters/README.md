Charming but suitable for high school, refined sculpted materials, expressive natural faces, sunny emerald and mint green with warm peach and cream accents. Horizontal group composition, all people and tree fully visible, isolated illustration with generous breathing room, transparent background. Crisp clean silhouette. No words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no QR, no watermarks, no extra hands. The final poster text and real QR will be typeset separately in an original page layout.
